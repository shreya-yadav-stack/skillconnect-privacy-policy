# SkillConnect Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 31, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to SkillConnect. This Privacy Policy explains how SkillConnect collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you use our mobile application and services.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use SkillConnect, we may collect:

- **Account information:** Name, email address, and password.
- **Authentication information:** Information received through Google Sign-In or Facebook Login when you choose those options.
- **Profile information:** Profile details, profile photos, and other information you provide.
- **Location information:** Address details and location coordinates when you use location detection or enter your address.
- **Membership information:** Selected membership plans and subscription status.
- **Usage information:** Information needed to operate and improve application features.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

- Create and manage user accounts.
- Authenticate users and provide secure access.
- Display user profiles and profile photos.
- Provide location-based features and address search.
- Manage membership plans and subscriptions.
- Provide customer support.
- Maintain and improve SkillConnect.

## 4. Third-Party Services

SkillConnect may use third-party services to provide its features:

- **Google Sign-In:** For Google account authentication.
- **Facebook Login:** For Facebook account authentication when available.
- **OpenStreetMap / Nominatim:** For address search.
- **MapLibre and OpenFreeMap:** For map display.
- **Storj:** For profile photo storage.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

SkillConnect stores account and application information using its backend services and database. Profile photos may be stored using Storj.

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate SkillConnect or when required by applicable law.

Profile information that users choose to make visible may be accessible to other users of the application.

## 7. Location Information

SkillConnect may use your device's location, with your permission, to detect your address and provide location-related features.

You can manage location permissions through your device settings.
